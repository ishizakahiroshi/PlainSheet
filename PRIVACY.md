# PlainSheet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

PlainSheet is a local-first editor for plain text table files. This policy
covers the PlainSheet desktop app (GitHub Releases, npm, winget and Microsoft
Store editions) and the PlainSheet web build.

## What PlainSheet collects

Nothing. PlainSheet does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal
information or any other data.

- **No account.** There is no sign-in and no registration.
- **No telemetry.** The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising
  or tracking code.
- **No uploads.** Files you open are read from your device and saved back to
  your device. Their contents are not sent anywhere.
- **No AI services.** The app does not call any AI or other online API.

## What stays on your device

- The files you open and save, at the locations you choose.
- Your settings (such as theme and language) and the list of recently opened
  files, kept in the app's local storage on your device. Removing the app's
  data or the app itself removes them.

## Network access

The desktop app does not need a network connection and its content security
policy does not allow connections to remote servers. The app does not check
for updates on its own; updates arrive through the channel you installed it
from (for example the Microsoft Store or a package manager), which operates
under its own privacy terms.

The web build is a static page. Opening a file in it reads the file inside your
browser; the file is not uploaded. The service that hosts the page may keep
standard access logs under its own privacy terms.

## Why the Microsoft Store listing says the app can access personal information

The Store edition is a packaged desktop app, and the Store marks every such
app as able to access personal information because it can read the files you
choose to open. PlainSheet uses that ability only to open and save those files
on your device, as described above.
